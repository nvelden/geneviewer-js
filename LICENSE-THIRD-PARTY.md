# Third-party material

## Font Awesome Free icons

The toolbar's default icons (download, rotate-left, rotate-right, house,
magnifying-glass-minus) are glyphs of Font Awesome Free 6.5.2 (solid), by
Fonticons, Inc., https://fontawesome.com. They are drawn as inline SVG paths,
in `src/lib/icons.js` and the built files in `dist/`.

The icons are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0):

https://fontawesome.com/license/free.
